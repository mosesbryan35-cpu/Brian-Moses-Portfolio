02 / About

Communicator.
Producer.
Storyteller.

Brian Mokua Moses is a Kenyan communications and multimedia professional based in Nairobi.

His work moves between research, writing, photography, video, audio and digital content. He has worked across educational and advocacy content, nature and tourism storytelling, public communication and creative production.

He is particularly interested in work that moves beyond the desk — into communities, landscapes, conversations and real situations where stories have context.
